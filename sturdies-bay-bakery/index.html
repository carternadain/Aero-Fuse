Our story

An island bakery where the ferry line becomes the coffee line.

We're a small, family-run bakery on Galiano Island. Every morning the ovens fire up early for bread, buns and pastries made by hand, in small batches, the slow way.

Locals, cyclists, campers and ferry travellers all end up at our counter sooner or later. Pull up a chair, smell the salt air, stay a while.
