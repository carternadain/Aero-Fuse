Sturdies Bay
Bakery

Bread, buns & coffee up the hill from the ferry.

A small family-run bakery at the Galiano ferry. Everything is baked here each morning: cinnamon buns, rustic loaves, scones, hand pies and whole pies, with espresso and drip coffee to go with them.

Visit

A short walk up Madrona Drive from the Sturdies Bay ferry terminal. Stop in before your sailing or on your way onto the island.
